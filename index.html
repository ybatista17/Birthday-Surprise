    How to look after her

    Blue Bahama is a hardy cultivar of the blue passionflower. It grows fast, climbs with curly tendrils, and flowers on new growth from early summer into fall. She can flower indoors, but only with plenty of light. In a city apartment, light is the thing to get right.
